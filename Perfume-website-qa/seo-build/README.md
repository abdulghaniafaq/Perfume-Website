# AEO / GEO: what was done, how it is kept true, and what only you can do (this folder is never uploaded)

AEO = answer engine optimisation (being the answer in Google's answer boxes, voice assistants, Bing/Copilot).
GEO = generative engine optimisation (being found and cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI answers).
Nobody can promise a result here: the engines do not publish how they choose sources. What follows is what the evidence supports.

## What the research says (November 2026)
- **Most AI crawlers do not run JavaScript** (OpenAI's, Anthropic's and Perplexity's, per independent crawl analyses; Google's does). The catalogue is drawn by JavaScript, so those crawlers saw an empty page where the 627 perfumes should be. This was the biggest gap, and `perfumes.html` closes it.
- **Google says no special markup is needed for its AI answers**; structured data must match what is visible; and a page must be indexed and allowed to show a snippet. FAQ rich results have been retired, so FAQ markup helps little in Google. It is kept because it costs nothing and is tested to match the page.
- **llms.txt is not used by the major AI providers** (Google says it has no effect; one study found 97% of such files get zero requests). It is included because it is cheap, not because it is proven.
- **What the academic work (Princeton, KDD 2024) found helps** a page get cited: clear statistics, cited sources, quotations. Hence the real numbers (627 perfumes, 89 houses, vials from ₹150, testers 10–30% cheaper) in plain text, and the cited court rulings in the testers guide.

## How it is kept true
`aeo.test.mjs` (part of `npm test`) fails if: a perfume or price on `perfumes.html` differs from `data/products-data.js`; the FAQ markup differs from the visible questions and answers; a number in the home page, `llms.txt` or the list differs from the data or `site-config.js`; any address in the markup is not a real page; the sitemap, a page's `dateModified` and a guide's "Updated" date disagree; `robots.txt` blocks anything; a page loses its snippet permissions; a guide loses its Quick answer.

### After you change products, prices or text
1. `node seo-build/build-all.mjs`  (one command: rebuilds the guides, `perfumes.html`, all 627 perfume pages, refreshes the structured data and footer links on the hand-written pages, and rewrites `sitemap.xml`)
2. Change `MODIFIED` in `guides-build/meta.mjs` and the dates in `sitemap.xml` when a page's text changes in a way a reader would notice.
3. `npm test` (the AEO, perfume-page and security tests will tell you if anything is out of step).

## What only you can do
1. **The site can be listed as soon as it is uploaded.** The old `_headers` file that told search engines to stay away was removed on 21 November 2026. A `_headers` file is back, but it only carries **security headers** (see `README.md`, "Security"); the launch checks in `aeo.test.mjs`, `site.audit.test.mjs` and `security.test.mjs` fail if it ever gains a `noindex` / `X-Robots-Tag` line. (For the record, the old file read: `/*` then `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`. Never put it back.)
2. **After going live**: add the site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, submit `sitemap.xml`, and use "Request indexing" for the home page. (IndexNow can ping Bing and others; ask and I will set it up.)
3. **Say who you are.** The strongest trust signals for both engines are real names and facts we do not have: who runs the shop, since when, the city, a GST or business registration number, an Instagram or Google Business Profile link (it goes in `sameAs`), and real customer reviews. Send these and I will add them to the About facts and the markup. Do not add anything that is not true.
4. **Be mentioned elsewhere.** AI answers lean on what other sites say about a business: a Google Business Profile, listings, reviews, a few articles or mentions. No change to this site can replace that.
5. **Product feed.** Google Shopping and ChatGPT shopping use product feeds, which need a barcode (GTIN) and live stock as well as a page per product. The site now has a page per perfume (`perfume-<slug>.html`), but no barcodes and no live stock, so the structured data deliberately leaves out availability, ratings and reviews rather than guess.

## Your choices
- **Training crawlers** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended) are allowed in `robots.txt`, so your guides can inform the models. To stay visible in AI search but opt out of training, change `Allow: /` to `Disallow: /` under those four.
- **The "Inspired by" references** on `perfumes.html` repeat what the catalogue cards already say, with the same disclaimer. If you ever remove them from the cards, remove them from the data and rebuild the list.

## How to check whether it is working (once the site is listable)
Each month ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google (AI Mode) these, and note whether Perfume X Collections is named or cited:
1. How can I tell if a perfume is original in India?
2. Which perfume is best for summer in India?
3. What is the difference between a perfume tester and a retail bottle?
4. Are perfume testers genuine?
5. Is Perfume X Collections legit?
6. Where can I buy Lattafa Khamrah in India?
7. Best online shop for Middle-Eastern perfumes in India
8. Perfume decant vs discovery set: which is better?
9. How long does delivery take at Perfume X Collections?
10. Does Perfume X Collections give a refund if a perfume is fake?
