3. How your order works

  1. You send your request on WhatsApp — from your cart, or with the Order button on any card.
  2. We confirm availability and your total.
  3. You make the payment and send us a screenshot on WhatsApp.
  4. We confirm we have received it. That is your order confirmation.
  5. Packing begins within 48 hours of that confirmation.
  6. Your order is generally shipped within 3–4 days, depending on which state in India you are in.
  7. The courier service sends you updates on the phone number you gave us.

4. Payment

Payment is made through WhatsApp, not on this website, and we never ask for card details here. After you pay, send us a screenshot; your order is confirmed only once we tell you we have received your payment. We will never ask for your OTP or banking password.

If you don't have trust yet, start small: order a mini vial (from ₹150). When it arrives you can see for yourself that the fragrance is genuine and authentic — and you are always welcome to order again.
