Questions, answered

What is Perfume X Collections?

Perfume X Collections is an independent online perfume retailer in India. We sell genuine designer, luxury and Middle-Eastern fragrances at insider prices, and take orders on WhatsApp instead of a website checkout. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the brands we list.

Are your perfumes really 100% genuine?

Yes. Every fragrance we list is officially sourced, and every bottle is 100% authentic and genuine. If you ever find otherwise, our 3× refund guarantee applies.

How does the 3× refund guarantee work?

Take the bottle to a reputed store — Sephora, Nykaa, Shoppers Stop or Parcos — and have it authenticated in person. If the store confirms in writing that it isn't genuine, send us the report on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery and we'll refund three times the amount you paid for that item. Independent labs, apps and other audits aren't accepted. Read the full claim rules.

What does “Inspired by” mean?

Where a card reads Inspired by, you are buying an original creation of the house named at the top of that card — it shares a scent profile with the designer fragrance named underneath, which is listed for reference only. It is a genuine product of that house, not a copy or repackaging of the designer original.

Why are your prices lower than in the shops?

Because our fragrances are officially sourced and passed on at insider prices, without the markup of a big retail operation. Same fragrance, no inflated price tag.

Which brands do you sell?

We carry 89 houses. Designer: Chanel, Dior, Versace, Gucci, Paco Rabanne and more. Luxury and niche: Tom Ford, Maison Margiela, Montale, Mancera and more. Middle Eastern: Lattafa, Rasasi, Armaf, Afnan, Swiss Arabian and more. See every perfume, A to Z, or browse the catalogue.

What's the difference between Retail and Tester?

A tester is the same fragrance as the retail bottle, usually supplied in a plain box (or none) and without retail packaging — which is why it costs less. Any packaging note, such as “No Box”, is shown on the card. Read the full guide to testers.

How do I place an order?

Add fragrances to your cart and press “Order on WhatsApp”, or tap Order on any card for a single item. Your selection arrives as a ready-made message. We'll confirm availability, the total and delivery with you there. See how ordering works.

How do I pay for an order?

Payment is made through WhatsApp, not on this website. We confirm availability and your total, you make the payment and send us a screenshot, and we confirm we have received it. That is your order confirmation. Read the terms.

Will you ask for my card details or OTP?

No. We never ask for card details on this website, and we will never ask for your OTP or banking password.

Do you deliver across India, and how long does it take?

Yes, we sell across India. Packing begins within 48 hours of your payment confirmation, and your order is generally shipped within 3–4 days, depending on which state you are in. The courier sends you updates on the phone number you gave us. Delivery times are estimates and can be affected by the courier, the weather or local conditions.

Are your prices in rupees, and are they up to date?

Prices are in INR and were last updated November 2026. Prices and stock can change, so we confirm availability and your final total with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

I'm not sure about ordering yet. Where should I start?

Start small: order a mini vial, from ₹150. When it arrives you can see for yourself that the fragrance is genuine and authentic, and you are always welcome to order again.

What if I can't find the perfume I'm looking for?

Send us a message with the name and size and we'll let you know what we can do.
