How ordering works

No checkout. No account.

Three steps, all on WhatsApp.

No. We confirm availability and the total with you on WhatsApp, and payment is settled directly with us.

Prices are in INR and subject to availability. They were last updated November 2026, and we confirm the final total with you before you pay.
