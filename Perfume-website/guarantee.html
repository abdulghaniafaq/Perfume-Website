Find a fake, and we refund you three times over.

We can make that promise because every fragrance we list is officially sourced. We don't deal in counterfeits, so we're not worried about the bill.

Making a claim

The audit must be done in person at a reputed store

Take the bottle to a well-known perfume retailer, anywhere in the world, and ask their staff to authenticate it — for example:

  • Sephora
  • Nykaa
  • Shoppers Stop
  • Parcos

A comparable reputed store, or the brand's own boutique, is fine too. Independent labs, online or app-based checks, other sellers, local shops and self-made reports are not accepted — no independent or fake audit will be entertained.

So every claim is genuine and quick to settle

  1. Tell us first. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number within 7 days of delivery, before the bottle goes to the store.
  2. Film the unboxing. Record one continuous, unedited video when you open the parcel, showing the sealed parcel and its label. Claims without it can't be accepted.
  3. A proper written report. On the store's letterhead or official system, dated, signed or stamped by a named staff member, with the store's address and phone number, the fragrance name, size and batch code, and a clear statement that the item is not genuine.
  4. We confirm it with the store. We may contact the store directly to check the report. A report the store doesn't confirm, or that has been altered, is rejected.
  5. It must be the bottle we sent. Original box, cap and batch code intact — not refilled, decanted, swapped or tampered with. We match the batch code with our dispatch records, and the bottle comes back to us for inspection before we pay out.
  6. One bottle, one report. Each report covers a single bottle from a single order. A report can't be reused or shared for another bottle or order.
  7. It has to say “not genuine”. “Unable to verify”, “we don't stock this brand” or a difference in packaging are not findings of a fake. Testers, minis and vials are as described in their listing, and a scent you don't like, that smells different on your skin or that fades sooner than you hoped is not a counterfeit.
  8. Fraud voids the guarantee. A forged, altered or reused report, or a swapped bottle, cancels the claim — and we may refuse future orders and report it to the authorities.
