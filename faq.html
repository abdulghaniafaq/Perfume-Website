Good to know

Questions, answered

Are your perfumes really 100% genuine?

Yes. Every fragrance we list is officially sourced, and every bottle is 100% authentic and genuine. If you ever find otherwise, our 3× refund guarantee applies.

How does the 3× refund guarantee work?

Have your purchase independently authenticated by any lab or expert you choose. If it's found not to be genuine, send us the result on WhatsApp and we'll refund three times the amount you paid for that item. Read the full guarantee.

What does “Inspired by” mean?

Where a card reads Inspired by, you are buying an original creation of the house named at the top of that card — it shares a scent profile with the designer fragrance named underneath, which is listed for reference only. It is a genuine product of that house, not a copy or repackaging of the designer original.

Why are your prices lower than in the shops?

Because our fragrances are officially sourced and passed on at insider prices, without the markup of a big retail operation. Same fragrance, no inflated price tag.

What's the difference between Retail and Tester?

A tester is the same fragrance as the retail bottle, usually supplied in a plain box (or none) and without retail packaging — which is why it costs less. Any packaging note, such as “No Box”, is shown on the card.

How do I place an order?

Add fragrances to your cart and press “Order on WhatsApp”, or tap Order on any card for a single item. Your selection arrives as a ready-made message. We'll confirm availability, the total and delivery with you there. See how ordering works.

I can't find the perfume I'm looking for.

Send us a message with the name and size and we'll let you know what we can do.
